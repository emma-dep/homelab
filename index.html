ABOUT THE LAB

Learning by building.

I'm Emma, an aspiring IT professional based in Belgium. I built this homelab to turn technical concepts into systems I could deploy, break, troubleshoot and improve myself.

What started as self-hosting a few services has grown into a multi-system Linux environment covering networking, containers, monitoring, backups, private PKI, automation and local voice processing.

This portfolio documents not only what I run, but why I designed it that way, the problems I encountered and what I learned solving them.
